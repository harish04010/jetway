---
title: Lost baggage claims
route: baggage
---
A bag that hasn't been found after five days is treated as lost. The customer then completes a lost-baggage claim listing the contents and their value.

Compensation follows the declared contents and receipts, up to $3,800 per passenger on domestic flights.

Claims are reviewed within 30 days. Any essentials already reimbursed while the bag was delayed are deducted from the final payment.

Agents should never promise an amount on social media. They can confirm that a claim was received and give its expected review date.
