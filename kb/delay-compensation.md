---
title: Delays and what we offer
route: disruption
---
We post delay updates in the app and by text as soon as we know about them.

For a delay of three hours or more that's within our control, such as crew or maintenance, customers get a meal voucher. For four hours or more, they also get 5,000 loyalty miles or a $75 travel credit, whichever they choose.

Delays caused by weather or air traffic control don't qualify for credits, but customers can still switch to an earlier or later flight on the same day at no charge.

Agents should give the cause of the delay if it's known and never guess at a departure time.
