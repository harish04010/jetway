---
title: Onboard problems
route: service
---
If seat power, wifi or entertainment didn't work on a flight, customers can request a refund of any wifi purchase and receive 2,500 loyalty miles for the inconvenience.

If a seat was broken and couldn't be reclined or used, we offer a $50 travel credit.

Customers can report onboard problems through the app's flight feedback form within 30 days.
