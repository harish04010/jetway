---
title: Missed connections
route: disruption
---
If a delay on our flight makes a customer miss a connection on the same booking, we rebook them on the next available flight automatically. They don't need to stand in line at the service desk; the new boarding pass appears in the app.

If the next flight is the following day and the delay was within our control, the hotel and meal policy for cancellations applies.

Connections booked on separate tickets aren't protected, but agents can offer a same-day standby seat when one is available.
