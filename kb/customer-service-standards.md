---
title: Social media response standards
route: service
---
Reply to every complaint within 30 minutes, in a friendly, plain tone. Use the customer's words back to them so they know they've been heard.

Never ask for confirmation codes, card details or phone numbers in a public reply. Ask the customer to send a direct message instead.

Apologize for the experience, not for policy. Don't blame other teams, airports or partners by name.

Keep public replies under 280 characters. If a case needs more than two replies, move it to direct messages.
