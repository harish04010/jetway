---
title: Changing or cancelling a booking
route: booking
---
Main cabin and higher fares can be changed with no change fee; the customer pays only any difference in fare. Basic fares can't be changed after 24 hours.

Any booking can be cancelled for a full refund within 24 hours of purchase if it was made at least seven days before departure.

After 24 hours, cancelled main cabin fares become a travel credit valid for 12 months.
