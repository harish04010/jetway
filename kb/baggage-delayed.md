---
title: Delayed baggage
route: baggage
---
If a bag doesn't arrive on the carousel, file a delayed-baggage report at the arrival airport's baggage office or online within 24 hours. The report gives you a file reference that starts with two letters and five digits.

Most delayed bags are found within 48 hours and delivered to the address on the report at no charge.

While a bag is delayed more than 12 hours, we reimburse reasonable essentials such as toiletries and a change of clothes, up to $50 per day for five days, with receipts.

You can track a bag with the file reference on the baggage tracking page. Social media agents can look it up if the customer sends the reference by direct message.
