---
title: Damaged baggage
route: baggage
---
Report damage at the airport before leaving, or online within 7 days of the flight. Photos of the damage and the bag tag help the claim.

We repair the bag through a partner shop or, if it can't be repaired, reimburse its depreciated value.

Normal wear is not covered: scuffs, scratches, small dents, and damage to wheels, handles or straps from ordinary handling. Damage to fragile items packed in checked bags is not covered either.
