---
title: Cancelled flights and rebooking
route: disruption
---
When we cancel a flight, we automatically rebook the customer on the next available flight to their destination and send the new itinerary by text and email.

If the new flight doesn't work, the customer can choose another flight within three days of the original date at no cost, or cancel and take a full refund to the original form of payment, even on non-refundable fares.

If a cancellation within our control causes an overnight stay, we provide a hotel voucher, transport to the hotel, and a meal voucher. Weather cancellations don't include a hotel, but agents can share discounted hotel rates.

Social media agents can rebook customers directly if they send their confirmation code by direct message.
